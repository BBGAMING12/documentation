Fort Worth Texas Roleplay (FWTXRP) is a FiveM-based GTA roleplay community dedicated to immersive, realistic law enforcement, fire department, and civilian roleplay set in Fort Worth, Texas. This site is your central reference for server rules, vehicle and weapon requirements, department regulations, and voice channel usage.

## Quick Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Community">
    Join the FWTXRP Discord server to get your Community Member role and access the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Server Rules">
    Read through the [server ranks](/server-ranks), [vehicle requirements](/vehicles/requirements), and [weapon requirements](/weapons/requirements) before playing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your Role">
    Decide whether you want to play as a civilian, join the [Fort Worth Police Department](/fwpd/overview), or the [Fire Department](/fire/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the Voice System">
    Familiarize yourself with the [/vc voice channel commands](/voice-channels) to communicate properly in-game.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Always follow the server rules and department regulations. Violations may result in kicks, bans, or removal from whitelisted departments.
</Note>
