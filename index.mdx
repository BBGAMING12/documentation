Fort Worth Texas Roleplay (FWTXRP) is a Roblox ERLC (Emergency Response: Liberty County) server for law enforcement, fire department, and civilian roleplay set in Fort Worth, Texas. This site covers server rules, vehicle and weapon requirements, department regulations, and voice channel usage.

## Quick Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Community">
    Join the FWTXRP Discord to get your Community Member role and server access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Server Rules">
    Read [server ranks](/server-ranks), [vehicle requirements](/vehicles/requirements), and [weapon requirements](/weapons/requirements) before playing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Your Role">
    Civilian, [Fort Worth Police Department](/fwpd/overview), or [Fire Department](/fire/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the Voice System">
    Get familiar with the [/vc voice channel commands](/voice-channels).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Follow server rules and department regulations. Violations can result in kicks, bans, or removal from whitelisted departments.
</Note>
