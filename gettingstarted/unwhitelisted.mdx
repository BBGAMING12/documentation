This page covers what's available to community members who have **not** yet been whitelisted into a department, plus everything you need once you're whitelisted into FWPD.


<Tabs>

<Tab title="Going On Duty">

Once you're whitelisted into the Police Department, follow these steps every time you go on duty.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the police team">
    Select the Police Department team in-game before spawning in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your uniform">
    Equip an approved uniform — see the Uniforms tab above for the full class breakdown and required gear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your loadout">
    Standard patrol loadout: Glock 19, Taser, Handcuffs, Pepper Spray, Citation Book. Your M4A1 stays in the trunk unless the situation requires it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spawn your car">
    Pull an approved patrol vehicle — see the Car Regulations tab above for approved models and equipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your Discord display name">
    Format your name as your call sign followed by your Roblox username, for example: `1A-120 | dpwfootball`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go 10-8 on RTO">
    Join the RTO voice channel and call yourself in as **10-8** (in service). Not sure what a code means? Check the [10-Codes](/comms/10-codes) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
