Our team has noticed that passive roleplay is often lacking during priority cooldowns. To enhance the experience, we've put together some ideas to help you stay engaged and make the most of your time on the server.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Everyday & Passive">

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Car Troubles" icon="car">
    Your car won't start, overheats, or breaks down in the middle of the road. Call a tow truck or try to fix it yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gas Station Stop" icon="fuel">
    Roleplay filling up your car, checking tire pressure, and maybe even arguing about gas prices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lost or Confused Person" icon="map-pin">
    A tourist, child, or elderly person asks for directions, or someone with dementia forgets where they live.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Neighborhood Dispute" icon="house">
    Argue with a neighbor over property lines, loud music, or a barking dog.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Street Vendor Roleplay" icon="store">
    Sell hot dogs, newspapers, flowers, or knock-off sunglasses to pedestrians.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public Transit Mishaps" icon="bus">
    Miss the bus, complain about delays, or witness someone causing a scene.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bar or Nightclub Scene" icon="martini">
    Socialize, dance, or have a disagreement over spilled drinks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suspicious Package" icon="package">
    Notice an unattended backpack in a public space and report it to the authorities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stranded Motorist" icon="octagon-alert">
    Run out of gas on the highway and flag down help.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pet Emergency" icon="dog">
    A dog gets loose and runs into traffic, or a cat is stuck in a tree.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

</Tab>

<Tab title="Minor Crimes & Suspicious Activity">

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Loitering" icon="footprints">
    Hang around a business or public space aimlessly, annoying shop owners or drawing suspicion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Jaywalking" icon="milestone">
    Crossing the street dangerously, causing drivers to honk or call 911.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Street Scams" icon="badge-dollar-sign">
    Sell fake concert tickets, perform card tricks, or try to "repair" a stranger's car for a quick buck.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public Intoxication" icon="wine">
    A drunk person stumbling around, slurring words, or making a scene.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drunk & Disorderly" icon="triangle-alert">
    Get into an argument while intoxicated, accidentally knock over trash cans, or annoy other pedestrians.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vandalism & Graffiti" icon="spray-can">
    Spray-paint walls, scratch messages into windows, or smash mailboxes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bribery" icon="hand-coins">
    Offer an officer money or goods to avoid a ticket or arrest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trespassing" icon="door-closed">
    Entering restricted areas like construction sites, private homes, or rooftops.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Illegal Dumping" icon="trash-2">
    Dispose of trash, old furniture, or hazardous waste in an unauthorized location.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stalking" icon="eye">
    Following someone around town for suspicious reasons.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

</Tab>

</Tabs>
