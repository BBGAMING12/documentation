Fort Worth Texas Roleplay (FWTXRP) is a FiveM GTA roleplay community built around immersive, realistic scenarios set in Fort Worth, Texas. Whether you want to patrol the streets as a law enforcement officer, respond to emergencies with the fire department, or live out everyday civilian life, FWTXRP gives you a dedicated community and structured environment to make every scene feel authentic.

## What Is FWTXRP?

FWTXRP runs on **FiveM**, a multiplayer modification framework for Grand Theft Auto V. The server is themed around Fort Worth, Texas, and emphasizes realistic, story-driven roleplay over casual gameplay. The community is organized into departments and civilian roles, each with its own standards, vehicles, and responsibilities.

## Roleplay Options

You can participate in three main roleplay paths on the server:

- **Law Enforcement (FWPD)** — Join the Fort Worth Police Department as a whitelisted officer. You will respond to calls, conduct traffic stops, manage active scenes, and work alongside other officers under department regulations.
- **Fire Department (FD)** — Serve as a whitelisted firefighter or EMS responder. You will handle emergency calls, coordinate with law enforcement, and follow department protocols.
- **Civilian** — Play as an everyday citizen of Fort Worth. Civilian roleplay includes anything from owning businesses and driving around town to interacting organically with active scenes.

<Note>
  All community members — regardless of role — are expected to read and respect every server rule. Violations may result in removal from whitelisted departments, kicks, or permanent bans.
</Note>

## How to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the FWTXRP Discord">
    Head to the official FWTXRP Discord server. This is where you receive announcements, interact with staff, and request roles. You must be in the Discord to access the FiveM server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Community Member Role">
    Once you join Discord, introduce yourself and obtain the **Community Member** role. This base rank gives you immediate access to the server and a core set of vehicles and weapons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Rules">
    Before your first session, read through the server ranks, vehicle requirements, and weapon requirements pages. Understanding what you can and cannot do from the start prevents rule violations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Path">
    Decide whether you want to play as a civilian right away or work toward a whitelisted department position. Whitelisted roles (FWPD and FD) require the **Whitelisted Member** rank, which is earned through the community.
  </Step>
</Steps>
