Every vehicle on the FWTXRP server has a minimum set of requirements you must meet before you can legally drive it. Some vehicles only require Community Member status, while others stack multiple conditions — make sure you satisfy every listed requirement before getting behind the wheel.

<Warning>
  Requirements **stack**. If a vehicle lists more than one requirement, you must meet **all** of them at the same time — satisfying only one is not enough to access that vehicle.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Not sure what the rank titles mean? Head over to the [Server Ranks](/server-ranks) page for a full explanation of each rank and how to earn them.
</Note>

## Community Member (Restricted Use)

All Community Members can access these vehicles, but each one comes with a strict location or behaviour restriction you must follow at all times.

| Vehicle | Requirement(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 4-Wheeler & ATV | Community Member | Only allowed at High Rock |
| Trackor | Community Member | Only allowed at Farms |
| Lawn Mower | Community Member | Only allowed on Grass |
| News Reporter Van | Community Member | Do not interfere with active scenes |
