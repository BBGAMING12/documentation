Every FWPD patrol vehicle must be properly equipped before you take it on the road. Vehicle regulations exist to ensure all units look consistent, operate safely, and are immediately recognizable as official FWPD vehicles during roleplay scenarios. If your vehicle is missing required equipment or running a non-approved livery, you are in violation of department policy.

<Warning>
  Using a **non-approved livery** or going on patrol with **missing required equipment** is a vehicle regulations violation. You are responsible for checking your loadout before every shift.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  All equipment must be equipped **before** you go on patrol. Check your vehicle loadout in-game before starting your shift to make sure everything is correctly installed.
</Tip>

## Standard Equipment

All approved FWPD patrol vehicles — regardless of model — must carry the following standard livery and equipment:

- **FWPD Black and White Livery**
- Integrity Lightbar
- Side Window Lights
- Pushbar
- Grille Lights
- Fog Lights
- Tail Light Flasher

### Falcon Interceptor (2013, 2017, 2019, 2024 Models)

The Falcon Interceptor is available in four model years and is used as both a standard patrol vehicle and an SUV unit. All models require the standard loadout. **SUV models** also require Rear Visor Lights.

<Note>
  **Rear Visor Lights** apply to the **Falcon Interceptor SUV models only**. Do not equip this item on non-SUV variants.
</Note>

- ✅ FWPD Black and White Livery
- ✅ Integrity Lightbar
- ✅ Side Window Lights
- ✅ Rear Visor Lights *(SUV models only)*
- ✅ Pushbar
- ✅ Grille Lights
- ✅ Fog Lights
- ✅ Tail Light Flasher
