The Fort Worth Police Department (FWPD) is the primary law enforcement department on the FWTXRP server. As an officer, you are responsible for maintaining order across the city — handling everything from routine traffic stops to high-speed pursuits and complex multi-unit scenes. The FWPD sets the standard for professional roleplay on the server, and every officer is expected to uphold that standard at all times.

## What Officers Do

As an FWPD officer, your day-to-day duties include:

- **Patrol** — Actively cruise designated areas of Fort Worth to deter crime and respond to calls.
- **Traffic Stops** — Pull over vehicles for violations, run plates, and issue citations or warnings.
- **Pursuits** — Engage in vehicle and foot pursuits in accordance with department pursuit policy.
- **Scenes** — Respond to and manage active crime scenes, accidents, and emergency calls alongside other units.

## Special Enforcement Unit (SEU)

The Special Enforcement Unit (SEU) is a specialized division within the FWPD. SEU officers have access to exclusive equipment and vehicles not available to standard patrol officers. Assignment to SEU is handled separately from standard FWPD onboarding.

<Note>
  The **FWPD High Vis Vest** is restricted to SEU officers only. If you are not assigned to SEU, you may not wear it under any circumstances.
</Note>

## How to Join the FWPD

The FWPD is a **whitelisted department**. You must hold the **Whitelisted Member** rank on the server before you can apply. Once you meet that requirement, follow the steps below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Earn the Whitelisted Member Rank">
    Meet the server's requirements and obtain the Whitelisted Member rank. You cannot apply to the FWPD without it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Through the Discord">
    Navigate to the FWTXRP Discord server and submit your FWPD application in the correct channel. Follow all instructions in the application template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Training and Onboarding">
    After your application is accepted, you will be scheduled for department training and onboarding. Attend all required sessions and pass any evaluations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow All FWPD Regulations">
    Once active, you are held to every FWPD regulation — including uniform standards, vehicle regulations, and conduct policies — at all times while on duty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
