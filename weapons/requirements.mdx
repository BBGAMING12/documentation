Every weapon in Fort Worth Texas Roleplay has a set of requirements you must meet before you can legally carry or use it in a roleplay scenario. Some weapons are open to all community members, while others are gated behind server booster tiers, whitelist status, or a combination of both. Review the table below carefully before picking up any firearm or melee weapon to make sure you qualify.

<Warning>
  Requirements **stack** — you must meet **every** listed requirement for a weapon simultaneously. For example, a weapon listed as *Server Booster x1 + Whitelisted Member* means you must hold both the Booster x1 role **and** the Whitelisted Member role at the same time. Satisfying only one condition is not sufficient.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Using a restricted weapon without the proper rank or access level is a **rulebreak** and will be treated as such by staff. Punishment can range from a formal warning to a permanent ban depending on severity and repeat offences. If you are unsure whether you qualify for a weapon, ask a staff member before using it.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **What is priority, and how do you use the Skorpian lawfully?** Priority is a server-wide declaration that signals an active, escalated conflict is taking place — typically called out in the appropriate in-game or Discord channel before a situation kicks off. If priority is **active** and you are a participant in that conflict, you may use the Skorpian offensively. If priority is **not** active, you may still carry the Skorpian, but you may only fire it in genuine self-defense — meaning you are reacting to a direct, immediate threat against your character. Initiating or escalating a fight with the Skorpian while priority is inactive is a rulebreak regardless of the roleplay context surrounding it.
</Tip>
